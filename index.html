The Problem

Exclusivity Without Privacy. Privacy Without Exclusivity.

For all its beauty, Northern Michigan has always asked you to pick one.

Resorts sell exclusivity, but only on their schedule — a spa slot shared with strangers, a pool deck that's never really yours, a hallway between you and everyone else staying there too. Rentals sell privacy, but nothing else — no one at the door, no one resetting the hot tub, just a lockbox code and a listing's broken promises.

Amíra is a boutique micro resort on a bluff above Michigan's western shore, near one of the region's quiet lakeside towns. It takes the exclusivity a resort sells you and makes it entirely your own. Your own spa, whenever you want it. Your own hot tub, never shared, never scheduled. No hallways, because there are none to share. The only things not entirely yours are the view outside, and the path that leads you home to it.

Origin & Meaning

To Love. To Look.

Amíra takes its name from two roots, woven together — ami, to love, and mira, to look. To love a place is to really see it: not glance past it on the way to somewhere else, but stop, and take it in.

It's why every residence is built around a carefully chosen view, rather than away from it. Even the walk in asks something of you: a stone path through the pines that has you seeing again before you've reached the door.

You start noticing things you wouldn't on a weekend built around checklists. How the light moves across the water, hour by hour. How the pines sound different at dusk than at dawn. How a view you've looked at a dozen times still surprises you on the thirteenth. That's not incidental. It's the whole idea.

The Vision

Every Season. Not a Weekend.

Amíra brings the caliber of the world's most exclusive retreats to a part of Michigan most people know only for eight weeks in July and August — reimagined for every season and built for real rest, not just a getaway.

The rest of the year, the crowds go home and the region gets quiet, the kind of quiet Amíra was built around. Fall turns the bluff to color. Winter settles the lake into flat silver, steam lifting off a private hot tub into air cold enough to see your breath, with skiing and snowshoeing just down the road. Spring arrives slowly, ice breaking up on the water and the woods coming back green in patches.

Summer is still the reason most people find this shore: the beaches, the small towns worth an afternoon, the region at its liveliest. But a getaway ends when the season does. Amíra doesn't wait for one to start.

The Residences

A Retreat of One's Own

Each residence stands alone, complete and private. No hallways to pass through. No neighbors to notice. Floor-to-ceiling glass connects every room to the lake or the woods just outside, so you're in the middle of the landscape without ever needing to step out into it.

Resort-Level Amenities, All to Yourself.

A king bed. A soaking tub, a steam shower, and a private heated spa, each built into the residence itself. Stone-accented bathrooms throughout, and daily housekeeping on request, so the last day feels as effortless as the first.

An adults-only retreat by design, with select weekends set aside for families.

Looking Ahead

The Grounds

A shared space for when you want one, never required. Down the road, a central gathering place will open alongside the residences: a fireplace lounge for slow evenings, a gym with scheduled classes, an indoor-outdoor space for music and quiet company, and in time, a pool of its own.

The residence remains yours alone. The grounds are simply there, if you choose them.

Design Language

Built to Belong.

Clean, modern forms in concrete and wood, built for this land and nowhere else. Every material was chosen to age well and settle into its surroundings.

Nothing around the residences has been manicured into submission. Native trees left standing. The terrain left uneven, not graded flat. The bluff shaped just enough to move through. It's restraint: building into the landscape, rather than over it.

At night, the residences glow softly from within — warm light against dark water, the only sign that something is there at all.
