The Gap

Between "Resort" and "Rental"

Existing luxury options in the region are resort-style — built around shared amenities like golf, ski, and spa. The fast-growing alternative is high-end vacation rentals, which don't offer hotel-grade service or standing. Nothing sits between them. That's the space Amíra is built for.

Option One

The Resort

Boyne Highlands, Bay Harbor, The Homestead — built around shared amenities like golf, ski, and spa. Full service, but little privacy or standalone character.

The Gap

Amíra

A private collection of standalone residences with hotel-grade service and no shared corridors — the intention of a resort, the privacy of a rental.

Option Two

The Rental

The fast-growing high-end vacation rental market — private and often well-appointed, but without daily housekeeping or any real hotel-grade standing.

The closest real-world comp: The Marlow, a newly launched cluster of standalone two-bedroom houses on Pleasantview Road, Harbor Springs — the same corridor as this site, between Boyne Highlands and Nub's Nob, currently taking bookings for fall/winter 2026, at rates running roughly $550/night in October up to $725/night in July (informal look, not verified). It's proof the "small group of private houses" format is buildable, financeable, and books at Amíra's target rate in this exact market. Its positioning is deliberately different, though — family- and dog-friendly, self-catering, no daily service — closer to a curated rental cluster than a serviced residence. Amíra is aimed at the adults-only, hotel-grade-service segment The Marlow isn't competing in.

Beyond the Residence

A Location That Doesn't Go Quiet Off-Season

The comps above skew to peak-summer bookings. What actually supports a year-round rate is what's already sitting within minutes of the site.

Winter

Boyne Highlands and Nub's Nob — two of the Midwest's top-rated ski resorts, both within about 2 miles, combining for 60+ runs and Donald Ross–designed golf come summer.

Summer

Petoskey State Park's mile of Lake Michigan beach and dunes, a short ride down the Little Traverse Wheelway — the 26-mile paved path connecting Harbor Springs to Charlevoix.

Year-Round

Harbor Springs' walkable downtown — galleries, boutiques, and waterfront dining — plus Harbor Springs Municipal Airport (KMGN), a full-service general aviation field minutes from the site for private and charter arrivals.

Directional, not exhaustive — the point for underwriting is that demand drivers exist in all four seasons, not just July and August, which is what the occupancy ramp below leans on.

Financing Structure
